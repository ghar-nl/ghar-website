Nostalgia

A collection of what home felt like.

Prints we did not design, chosen because they feel like home.

These are pieces we bought from the artisan entrepreneurs we work with: prints that remind us of the homes we lived in back in India.

They are revived prints, and they hold the joy, the protection and the memories of the homes we grew up in, and of the spaces that have felt like home since.

Each one is hand block printed by a small artisan-run workshop, from their own designs, in a small batch. When a design is gone, it is gone.

Hello, for the first time

Hi — we're Dimple & Akkriti.

Two friends in Amsterdam, both originally from India, starting a very small business out of a shared love: hand block printed and hand woven textiles made slowly, in small batches, by artisans we work with directly.

We've spent the last few months building Ghar from nothing but an idea and a lot of fabric samples on our living room floor — and today, we get to say the words we've been waiting to say: our website is live.

"We're so excited to finally share this with you. Have a look around, and tell us what you think — good, bad, or somewhere in between."

Take a look at Ghar
What you'll find there
Hand block printed cushions — five prints, five stories, printed by hand in India.
Our first Ikat collection — wall art, table linens, and a cushion cover scaled all the way up.
The craft behind it — the 2,000-year traditions, the makers, and why we do this slowly.

Thank you for being here at the very start of this. It means more than you know.

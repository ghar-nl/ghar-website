Shipping info.

Where we deliver, what it costs, and how we pack every order.

We currently deliver within the EU only, shipping from Amsterdam.

Shipping is a flat €5 per order, and free on orders above €59.99.

All orders are pre-orders while we build our inventory, so delivery can take a few extra days. We will email you your exact delivery timeline as soon as your order ships.

We package every order with great care and responsibility — using recyclable packaging wherever we can, so what arrives at your door is as thoughtful as what is inside it.

Questions about your order or delivery? Write to us any time.

Help

Privacy & cookies.

What we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

Ghar is a trade name of Dim in Dam, a sole proprietorship registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KVK 99282771) in Amsterdam. Questions about your data? Write to dimple@ghar.nl.

What we collect, and why

When you place an order, contact us, or sign up for a workshop or news, we receive the details you type in: your name, email address and, for orders, your delivery address and what you ordered. We use them only to handle your order, answer you, or tell you about the workshop you signed up for. We never sell your data.

These details are stored in our own Google account (Google Sheets), which we use to run the shop. When you pay online, your payment is handled by our payment provider and we never see your card details.

We keep order records for seven years, because Dutch law requires businesses to keep their administration that long. Other messages and sign-ups we delete when they are no longer needed, or sooner if you ask.

Website statistics and cookies

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits to our pages without cookies and without building a profile of you. We see totals, such as how many people visited a page and which country they came from.

Google Analytics only runs if you click "Accept" in the cookie banner. It places cookies to help us understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are read most. Google processes this data, possibly outside the EU. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

Your shopping bag is remembered in your own browser (local storage), so it is still there when you come back. It is not sent to us until you order.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. Just email dimple@ghar.nl. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

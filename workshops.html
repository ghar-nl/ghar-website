Press. Lift. Move along. Repeat.

What you get

Everything is ready and waiting.

Come as you are. We have the blocks, the colour, the chai and a story to begin with. Tap any card to read a little more.

  • These are generous, sturdy totes made of thick 100% pure cotton. They are built to be used for years, so the pattern you press today goes to market, to the beach and to every picnic after.

    Print one, or print two. Both are yours to keep.

  • Every one of our hand-carved wooden blocks is on the table. Choose a favourite, mix a few, and build a pattern that is entirely your own.

    Meet the blocks

  • A whole palette is laid out for you to choose from. Go with one colour you love, or let two shades meet on the same cloth and see what happens.

  • Brushes of different kinds, and little tools to lay the colour on evenly and cleanly. They help every press come out crisp, and your tote come out neat.

  • Before the first press, we gather for a beautifully illustrated introduction to block printing, made especially for this workshop. It is full of stories: where block printing comes from, how a block is carved, what the patterns mean, and how to look after what you print.

    Indian block-printed cloth was being sold in Egypt about a thousand years ago. Centuries later, ships of the Dutch East India Company carried Indian prints to Amsterdam.

    Come for the stories. Stay, and watch your hands join them.

  • There is no rush here. Press, lift, look, and press again. Homemade chai, coffee and vegetarian snacks are served along the way, and other drinks are available from the venue's menu.

  • At the end, we set aside time to clean the blocks, and we will show you how. Trust us, it turns out to be a calming ritual in itself.

  • Your finished tote, or both of them, goes home with you: a pattern pressed by your own hands, ready for everyday life.

How the session unfolds

A gentle five-part rhythm.

Here is how the workshop flows, from the first cup of chai to taking your tote home.

  1. Arrive

    Find a seat and settle in with a cup of homemade chai.

  2. Listen

    The illustrated story of block printing opens the workshop.

  3. Print

    Choose your blocks and colours, and press at your own pace.

  4. Clean

    Together we clean the blocks, a quiet and calming ritual.

  5. Home

    Your tote, or two, goes home with you.

Good to know

A few questions.

Do I need any experience?

None at all. If you can press a stamp, you can block print. We guide you through every step, and there is no wrong way to make a pattern.

Can I come with friends?

Yes, please do. Each person books their own seat, so everyone in your group has their name on the list.

What does my ticket include?

Your ticket is €45 per person, incl. 21% VAT. It includes printing up to two tote bags, all of our blocks, colours, brushes and tools, the illustrated opening session, and homemade chai, coffee and vegetarian snacks. Other drinks are available from the venue's menu. The workshop runs for three hours, from 12:00 to 15:00.

I have a food allergy. Can you help?

Of course. All our food is homemade and vegetarian, and we are happy to make changes. When you book, write any food allergies in the box at checkout, or email us at dimple@ghar.nl.

What if I can't make it?

We are just starting out, and cancellations are hard for us to manage, so tickets are non-refundable. If you can't make it, please contact us and we will sign you up for our next workshop in January instead. We will have several options for you to choose from.

Where does it take place?

At De Steek Amsterdam, Van der Hoopstraat 70HS, 1051 VK Amsterdam, in Amsterdam West.

I have a question. Who do I ask?

We would love to hear from you. Message us on Instagram at @ghar_dam or write to dimple@ghar.nl.

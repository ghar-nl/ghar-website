Directions

At our stall

What you will find.

The whole collection

Ikat table runners, table mats and napkins, block printed cushion covers, and our everyday tote bags. Every piece is 100% pure cotton.

Try block printing

Pick up one of our hand-carved wooden blocks, press it onto cloth and watch the pattern appear. It takes a minute, and it is the best way to understand the craft.

Meet the makers' story

Ask us anything. We love telling you who made each piece, which block it came from and how the colour got there.

Gifts

We will have amazing gift sets for you, made by hand and at very reasonable prices.

Before the market

Block printing workshop, Saturday 5 December.

Want more time with the blocks? Join our workshop the day before the market, at Kringloop West in Amsterdam West from 11:00, and print your own tote bag. €40 per person (special launch price, very limited spots): everything included, and your first drink from the café is on us.
